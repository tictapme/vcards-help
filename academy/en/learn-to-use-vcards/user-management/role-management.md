# Role management

You can **edit the roles of team members** to decide what kind of **permissions** these members have. <br> <br>
To manage team member roles, follow these steps:

1. Access the **Users** section from your team's dropdown menu. <br> <br>
[![Dropdown 1.png](https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2024-06/scaled-1680-/YM3T5TeBOpkNtx3z-dropdown-1.png)](https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2024-06/YM3T5TeBOpkNtx3z-dropdown-1.png) <br> <br>
2. Click on the **user tag**, and choose the preferred role type for the user: <br> <br>
**Administrator**: Can manage team members, in addition to what a normal member can do. <br> <br>
**Normal**: Can manage labels, apps, templates, and cards. <br> <br>  [![marketing.png](https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2024-06/scaled-1680-/Hj2YvZVk1JONVpys-marketing.png)](https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2024-06/Hj2YvZVk1JONVpys-marketing.png) <br> <br>

All set! You have successfully modified the role of a user in your team. 🎉  <br> <br>
