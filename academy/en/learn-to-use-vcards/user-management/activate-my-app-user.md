# Activate my app user

In this article, we will guide you step-by-step to **activate your account and access your vcard**. This process includes initial activation from an email, acceptance of terms and conditions, creation of an unlock pattern, and finally, accessing and setting up your account. Follow each of the steps described below with their respective images to complete the activation of your user in a simple and quick manner. <br /> <br />
