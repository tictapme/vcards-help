# Consult form data

To view submitted forms from your app, go to the left sidebar menu and select *Activity* > *Inbox:* <br /><br /> 
[![Actividad.png](https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2024-07/scaled-1680-/3zi4iZyjql3aqVq8-actividad.png)](https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2024-07/3zi4iZyjql3aqVq8-actividad.png) <br /><br />  

Here you will find all the forms submitted by your team. You can filter them by date, form, or category:

[![Bandeja de entrada (1).png](https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2024-06/scaled-1680-/hRQ8uQkOCiJyAvix-bandeja-de-entrada-1.png)](https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2024-06/hRQ8uQkOCiJyAvix-bandeja-de-entrada-1.png) <br /><br />

☝️  Please note that you will only see forms if you have created a form in your app and if forms have been submitted.
