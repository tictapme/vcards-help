# Consult reports

To review the reports for each of your cards and brands, navigate to *Activity* > *Reports*. <br><br>
[![Analiticas.png](https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2024-07/scaled-1680-/gP5duIikLGRgxul1-analiticas.png)](https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2024-07/gP5duIikLGRgxul1-analiticas.png)
 <br><br>

In this section, you can observe the number of visits, the number of submitted forms, and the last visit date for each card or brand. Here, you can understand how your company's vcards are used, such as which departments use them the most.

[![Informes (3).png](https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2024-06/scaled-1680-/XbI8EO5nE9g4X9C6-informes-3.png)](https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2024-06/XbI8EO5nE9g4X9C6-informes-3.png) <br><br>

If you want to access the complete report for each card, click on the "View report" button. Here, you will have a more detailed report on the card's analytics, such as Visits by language, Visits by device, or Visits by browser. <br><br>

[![Informe (1).png](https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2024-06/scaled-1680-/hIbFf9aNg3lMiAhc-informe-1.png)](https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2024-06/hIbFf9aNg3lMiAhc-informe-1.png)
