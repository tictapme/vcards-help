# Manage the multimedia gallery

To access your team's multimedia gallery, navigate from the left sidebar menu. Here you can view all the images you've uploaded for your templates. From this section, you have the option to delete or download these images, as well as add new ones.
<br /><br />

<br /><br />

☝️ Keep in mind that deleting an image from the multimedia gallery will remove it from all templates where it's currently being used.
