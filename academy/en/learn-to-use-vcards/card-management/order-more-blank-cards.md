# Order more blank cards

<br>
<br>

<span class="cabezera">Here you can create cards without needing to add any information.
You can add it later, whenever you need to.
It’s important to fill in the details before printing the cards.</span>

<br>
<br>

<span class="color">1.</span> From the Cards section, click the **I want more cards button**, you’ll find it in the top left corner:

  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-04/scaled-1680-/aDHgUShf9LZBG1LE-quiero-mas-tarjetas-en.png" alt="quiero-mas-tarjetas_EN.png">
</div>

<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>

<div class="step-container-dividido">
  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-04/scaled-1680-/0RXiKpPZTOzlMdPC-panel-seleccion-tarjetas-digitales-en.png" alt="panel-seleccion-tarjetas-digitales_EN.png">
  <div class="step-text">
    <span class="color">2.</span> The following panel will open, and we’ll click on “Digital Cards”.
  </div>
</div>

<br>
<br>

<div class="step-container-dividido">
  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-04/scaled-1680-/yttCxi63SZ0UfRsg-image-1745507723211.png" alt="panel-seleccion-tarjetas-digitales-blancox2.png">
    <div class="step-text">
      <span class="color">3.</span><b> Blank cards:</b> They are created instantly, and you can edit the details after requesting the new cards.
  </div>
</div>
<br>
<br>

<span class="color">4.</span> Choose the <b>quantity and brand</b> of the new cards. Once you generate them, they’ll be ready in just a few seconds!
<div class="step-container">
  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-04/scaled-1680-/uLEfsGIqReXVoyb3-image-1745507761423.png" alt="panel-seleccion-digitales-cantidad-marcax2.png">
</div>
<br>
<br>

<span class="color">5.</span> If you want to modify the data, <u class="link">[you can do it using this guide.](https://help.tictapcards.com/books/card-management/page/edit-the-text-fields-of-a-vcard)</u>
