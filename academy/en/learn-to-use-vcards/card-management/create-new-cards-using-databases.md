# Create new cards using databases

<br />
<br />
<span class="cabezera"> You can complete all the information using a database, use your own or download our template.</span>

<br />
<br />

<span class="color">1.</span> From the **Cards section**, click the I **want more cards button**, you'll find it in the top left corner.
<div class="step-container-small">
  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-04/scaled-1680-/aDHgUShf9LZBG1LE-quiero-mas-tarjetas-en.png" alt="quiero-mas-tarjetas_EN.png" />
</div>
<br />
<br />

<div class="step-container-dividido">
  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-04/scaled-1680-/0RXiKpPZTOzlMdPC-panel-seleccion-tarjetas-digitales-en.png" alt="panel-seleccion-tarjetas-digitales_EN.png" />
  <div class="step-text">
    <span class="color">2.</span> The following panel will open, and we will click on <b>"QR Digital Cards"</b>
  </div>
</div>

<br />
<br />
<br />
<br />

<div class="step-container-dividido">
  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-04/scaled-1680-/o3Tjoe6XnJkt6vYe-panel-seleccion-digitales-importar-bd-en.png" alt="panel-seleccion-digitales-importar-bd_EN.png" />
  <div class="step-text">
     <span class="color">3.</span> Now let's <b>import</b> the cards from a <b>database.</b>
  </div>
</div>

<br />
<br />
<br />
<br />

<span class="color">4.</span> The first step is to select <b>the brand</b> for the cards.

<div class="step-container-small">
  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-04/scaled-1680-/G9oB4Q6encyd5Olz-panel-seleccion-digitales-bd-importar-en.png" alt="panel-seleccion-digitales-bd-importar_EN.png" />
</div>
<br />

<span class="color">5.</span> We can also drag and drop or select the existing database from a folder.
<br />
Keep in mind that the <b>format must be .CSV</b>

<div class="step-container">
  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-04/scaled-1680-/x6d2huguF4luVTDd-image-1745506870116.png" alt="panel-seleccion-digitales-bd-importar_EN.png" />
</div>
<br />
<br />

<span class="color">6.</span> If you don’t have a database, you can download our <b>template</b> and fill it in with your data.

<div class="step-container-small">
  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-04/scaled-1680-/NGU0GLETMvnvWm90-image-1745506997588.png" alt="panel-descargar-plantilla-bdx2.png" />
</div>

<br />
<br />

<span class="color">7.</span> We verify that the file and the brand are correct and proceed to the next step.
<div class="step-container">
  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-05/scaled-1680-/ILSjUFXAJtEir8X5-image-1747058546272.png" alt="[panel-digitales-bd-validacionx2.png"  />
</div>
<br />
<br />
<br />

<span class="color">8.</span> Now let’s check if everything is correct with the <b>data preview and validation</b>, we don’t want any surprises!
<div class="step-container">
  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-04/scaled-1680-/2jVxPTWcJ37OAQlT-image-1745507028808.png" alt="[panel-digitales-bd-validacionx2.png"  />
</div>
<br />
<br />
<br />

You're good to go!
