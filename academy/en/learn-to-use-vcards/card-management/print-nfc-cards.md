# Print NFC cards

<br>
<br>
Once you have your digital cards with all the information verified, you just need to follow these steps to print the vCards you need.
<br>
<br>

<span class="color">1.</span> **From the Cards section**, click on the I **want more cards button** — you'll find it at the top right.

<div class="step-container-small">
  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-04/scaled-1680-/aDHgUShf9LZBG1LE-quiero-mas-tarjetas-en.png" alt="quiero-mas-tarjetasx2.png">
</div>
<br>
<br>

<span class="color">2.</span> The following panel will open, select **"Print NFC cards"**.

<div class="step-container">
  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-04/scaled-1680-/3Mw16EUFV9zcUawR-image-1745508488490.png" alt="[panel-seleccion-fisicasx2.png">
</div>
<br>
<br>

<span class="color">3.</span> Now let's print the existing cards by clicking the <b>"Select cards"</b> button.

<div class="step-container">
  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-04/scaled-1680-/UnigymkgJi7ITIXi-image-1745508519830.png" alt="panel-seleccion-fisicas-sleccion-cardsx2.png">
</div>
<br>
<br>

<span class="color">4.</span> Select the <b>digital cards you want to print</b>.

<div class="step-container-big">
  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-04/scaled-1680-/pAEvFUEuGxM8UnOG-image-1745508554615.png" alt="panel-seleccion-fisicas-seleccion-fisicas-2x2.png">
</div>
<br>
<br>

<span class="color">5.</span> Once we finish selecting, we will go to the <b>data preview</b> to verify the printing details. Take your time and <b>verify</b> that everything is correct, as <b>the data will be used on the physical cards</b>.

<div class="step-container-big">
  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-04/scaled-1680-/BtWQ1hhFg0PnsWrQ-image-1745508576683.png" alt="panel-seleccion-fisicas-visualizacion-cardsx2.png">
</div>
<br>
<br>

<span class="color">6.</span> If you need to <b>modify</b> any data, you can go back to the <b>cards page</b> and make the changes there.
<br>
<u class="link">[Guide to make changes to vCards](https://help.tictapcards.com/books/card-management/page/edit-the-text-fields-of-a-vcard)</u>

<div class="step-container-big">
  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-04/scaled-1680-/TzhQpdAJ6lAnV91J-image-1745509145596-cards-en.png" alt="panel-fisicas-confirmar-pedidox2.png">
</div>
<br>
<br>

<span class="color">7.</span> If there is anything we <b>should be informed about</b>, don't hesitate! Send us whatever you need, such as the shipping address, in the <b>comments section</b>.

<div class="step-container">
  <img src="https://help.tictapcards.com/uploads/images/gallery/2025-04/scaled-1680-/man3441zIshdYtSI-image-1745509261337.png" alt="panel-fisicas-confirmar-pedidox2.png">
</div>

<br>
<br>

<span class="color">8.</span> Now that we have all the data ready for printing, all that's left is to <b>accept the terms and conditions</b> of the order and everything will be ready! ✨
<br>
