# Do I need to install an app?

No, our solution is completely appless, ensuring that you don't need to download or install any applications on your device. Your vcard resides securely in the cloud as a unique URL. This URL can be easily saved to your desktop or bookmarked in your browser, allowing for quick and convenient access at any time. 

Whether you need to share your contact information with someone or retrieve it for your own use, our cloud-based vcard ensures that your information is always available and up-to-date without the need for any additional software.
